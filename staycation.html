Lagoon Amusement Park

Lagoon is the last family-owned amusement parks in the nation, according to its owners, the Freed family. “That’s something really special to us. We don’t want to be corporate,” Julie Freed says. They’ve rebuffed sales pitches from larger amusement companies; if they sold out, the older rides would likely disappear, she says. And a perk that’s been in place since Lagoon’s beginning would end: It claims to be the only park in the nation that allows guests to bring in their own food and drinks, including alcohol.

Why you should visit:

  • Thrilling rides like Cannibal, Colossus, and Roller Coaster!
  • Lagoon, Beach, a full water park included with admission!
  • Family friendly areas with games, food, and entertainment!
  • Convenient location near Salt Lake City with plenty of nearby hotels and dining options.

Lagoon has been really entertaining guests for more than 100 years now, making it one of the oldest amusement parks in the United States. Many people return year after year because there’s always something new to enjoy and have fun with. It’s the perfect place for families, friends, and mechanic games lovers of all ages.
